Data Sources

This visualization compiles data from multiple verified sources:

  • K-12 School Shooting Database - Every US K-12 school shooting since 1970
  • The Violence Project - Mass Shooter Database with demographic data
  • Gun Violence Archive - Near real-time US gun violence data
  • Washington Post - Comprehensive US school shooting database
  • Wikipedia - Comprehensive lists of school shootings globally
  • World Population Review - Country comparison data

Definitions

School shooting: An incident where a firearm was discharged at a school, university, or educational institution, resulting in casualties or intended casualties.

Perpetrator demographics: Race/ethnicity classifications follow US Census categories. Trans status is based on available reporting and self-identification where known.

Dataset Integration

This final dataset combines 445+ incidents from multiple sources:

  • Washington Post data: Comprehensive US incidents but with limited race detail (many "Other"/"Unknown")
  • Curated data: Manually researched incidents with improved demographic accuracy
  • International data: Major incidents from global sources including terrorist attacks at schools

Trans vs Cisgender Analysis

The trans/cis comparison uses conservative estimates:

  • Only perpetrators with confirmed trans status are classified as transgender
  • US trans population estimate: 1.6% (Pew Research, 2022)
  • Analysis focuses on US incidents where demographic data is most complete
  • Per-capita rates use 2020 Census population data

Limitations

  • This visualization shows major incidents with detailed data. Additional incidents exist but lack comprehensive demographics.
  • International data is less complete than US data due to reporting differences.
  • Some historical coordinates are approximate.
  • Terrorist attacks at schools (Beslan, Peshawar, Garissa) are included but noted separately as they differ in motive and scale.
  • Race/ethnicity data quality varies by source and time period.

Interactive Features

Timeline: Use the timeline slider to filter by year range or press play for an animated progression.

Heatmap: Toggle to show incident density overlays on the globe.

Comparison Mode: Compare two demographic groups side-by-side with color-coded markers and statistics.

Filters: Use the filter panel to narrow down incidents by country, demographics, school type, or time period.

Note on Completeness

No dataset of school shootings is fully complete. This visualization prioritizes accuracy over comprehensiveness. If you notice an error or missing incident, the data methodology is documented for verification.
